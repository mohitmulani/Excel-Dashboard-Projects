\# Excel Dashboard Projects | Data Analytics Portfolio



\## Project Overview



This repository showcases two interactive Excel dashboard projects developed using Microsoft Excel.



The projects demonstrate how raw business data can be transformed into meaningful insights through data analysis, Pivot Tables, data summarisation and interactive dashboard visualisations.



The dashboards cover two different business scenarios: \*\*Business Sales Analysis\*\* and \*\*Production Analysis\*\*.



\## Tools \& Technologies



\- Microsoft Excel

\- Pivot Tables

\- Pivot Charts

\- Excel Formulas

\- Data Analysis

\- Data Visualisation

\- Interactive Dashboards



\---



\## Project 1: Business Sales Analysis Dashboard



\### Overview



This dashboard analyses business transaction data to understand financial performance across different regions, departments, product lines and customer segments.



\### Dataset Information



\- Total Records: 2,000

\- Transaction Date

\- Revenue

\- Expenses

\- Profit

\- Category

\- Region

\- Department

\- Product Line

\- Customer Segment

\- Payment Method



\### Key Analysis Areas



\- Revenue and profit analysis across product lines.

\- Regional business performance comparison.

\- Department-wise revenue, expenses and profitability.

\- Payment method distribution and profit contribution.

\- Average expenses across departments.

\- Transaction volume across different regions.



\### Business Objective



To provide a consolidated view of business performance and identify patterns in revenue, expenses and profitability that can support better business decision-making.



\*\*File:\*\* `Excel Dashboard 1.xlsx`

\## Project 2: Production Analysis Dashboard



\### Overview



This dashboard focuses on production performance, analysing manufacturing data across different product types, regions and managers.



\### Dataset Information



\- Total Records: 120

\- Production ID

\- Production Date

\- Region

\- Manager

\- Product Type

\- Units Produced

\- Total Cost

\- Cost Per Unit

\- Gender

\- Age



\### Key Analysis Areas



\- Production costs across different product types.

\- Manager-wise production activity.

\- Units produced over different time periods.

\- Average cost per unit across product categories.

\- Production performance across different regions.



\### Business Objective



To understand production trends, evaluate cost distribution and analyse manufacturing activity to support operational planning and cost management.



\*\*File:\*\* `Excel Dashboard 2.xlsx`
